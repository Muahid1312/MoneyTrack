# MoneyTrack 4.0 UI redesign

The screenshots referenced in the brief did not arrive with the written attachment. The redesign follows the supplied description of a quiet background, rounded cards, deep accent surfaces, compact navigation, and large editable amounts.

## Main flows

- **Home** shows the selected currency’s personal wallet cash, the current month’s income and expenses, and a Monday–Sunday spending chart. Tap a day to inspect that day’s expenses. Budget previews and recent transactions use the same currency. Shortcuts open expense, income, transfer, Group Funds and Managed Funds.
- **Add transaction** has a large amount field, category chips and an accessible numeric keypad. The keypad follows the wallet’s precision, and holding Delete clears the amount. Wallet, date and the full category list are available alongside the note. More details retains receipts, project assignment and recurring schedules. The star reuses a previous entry as a draft.
- **Budgets** shows the reporting-currency monthly limit, its donut and progress, and wallet-specific plans in their own currency and period. Wallet plans keep their rollover and custom-date rules. The overall limit and wallet plans are independent.
- **Accounts** groups balances by original currency. Wallet details show cash, available cash and reserved savings. Reconcile balance compares the recorded and counted amounts and opens the existing wallet history.
- **Group Funds** displays collected, spent and remaining money in the shared design. Member contribution and settlement views use the existing group ledger and refund allocation.
- **Managed Funds** displays live funding, spending and cash on the list and fund detail. Existing purchases, receipt actions and advance repayments use the new theme. Generate report opens the read-only activity report with PDF and CSV export. English and Dari report content use the selected language. Dari has right-to-left PDF layout.
- **More** keeps the searchable directory and pins. Transaction calendar, statistics, reports, profile, appearance, security, backups and the other finance tools are reachable there.

The four primary tabs are Home, Budgets, Accounts and More. The calendar retains its existing daily, weekly, monthly and total views. Shared component and Material styles apply to secondary routes and the device lock screen. Dari switches layout direction app-wide, and the redesigned primary screens have Dari labels. Some existing feature detail screens retain English labels.

The Managed Funds shopping list and settlement posting operations remain a separate development phase. Wallet reconciliation in this redesign compares balances and shows the difference. Audited adjustment postings and reconciliation history belong to the reconciliation phase.
