## Calendar geometry

The diagram draws the labeled ScreenQA bounds at their original positions. Unannotated areas are blank. The right side shows MoneyTrack’s populated calendar. The source captures include Android system bars. The widget render contains application content only. The viewport aspect ratios and text settings also differ. [Calendar layout comparison](screenshots/calendar-reference-layout.png)

At 1080 × 1920, the Calendar selector spans x=216–432. The income and total value cells span x=0–360 and x=720–1079. In the 390-pixel MoneyTrack viewport those same features occupy the second fifth and the outside thirds respectively. Both interfaces place the month, selector, summary, weekday row and dated entries in that order. This establishes placement and proportions for the measured elements. Exact current colors, typography and animation require contemporary screen captures.
